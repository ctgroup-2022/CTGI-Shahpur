    Have a question? Check out the FAQ

    What programs do CT Group of Institutions offer?

    CT Group of Institutions offer a wide range of Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Diploma programs in various fields including Engineering, Business Management, Computer Science (Cyber Security, AI/DS), Hotel Management, Education, Beauty & Aesthetic and Commerce

    How can I apply to CT Group of Institutions?

    You can apply to CT Group of Institutions through our online application portal. Visit our website and fill out the application form. You will need to upload your academic documents and recent photographs.

    What are the eligibility criteria for admission?

    A student must complete his/her 12th exam from affiliated board with minimum marks as set by the regulatory authority or into the second year of the course in Engineering or other courses. For PG, student must have completed graduation from affiliated board with minimum marks as set by the regulatory authority

    Are there any scholarships available?

    Yes, CT Group of Institutions offer scholarships to meritorious students based on academic performance, financial need, and other criteria. For more information, visit the scholarship page on our website.

    What are the hostel facilities at CT Group of Institutions?

    CT Group of Institutions has facility of Boys and Girls Separate Hostels, Gym, Laundry, Swimming Pool Etc with 24*7 Electricity Backup.

    How is the placement assistance at CT Group of Institutions?

    CT Group of Institutions has a dedicated placement cell that works tirelessly to secure job opportunities for students. It has Placement Preparatory program from first year onwards. We have collaborations with top companies, and our students have been successfully placed in reputed organizations.

    CT Group of institutions is among the best colleges for graduation and post graduation degrees in Jalandhar, Punjab.
